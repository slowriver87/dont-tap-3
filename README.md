# Don't Tap the 3

A two-minute phone game about attention. Part of Mind Lab: https://slowriver87.github.io/mindlabfun/

Numbers flash one at a time. Tap for every number except 3. Ten threes are hidden among 90 numbers.

## What it shows

- **3s you stopped on**: the main score. Tapping a 3 is a slip into autopilot.
- **Numbers you missed**: numbers you should have tapped.
- **Speed before slips**: people tend to tap faster just before a slip, a sign of autopilot.
- **First half vs second half**: whether your focus faded.
- **Chart** of every tap's speed with each 3 marked, plus a plain-English explanation.
- **History** of your last 20 runs, saved in your browser only.

## Limits

Only 10 threes, so one slip moves the score by 10%. Lab versions use over 200 numbers.

## Background

The Sustained Attention to Response Task: Robertson et al. (1997); Christoff et al. (2009); Killingsworth and Gilbert (2010); Mackworth (1948); Stothart, Mitchum and Yehnert (2015).
